About Search for Gaza

A search engine that donates 100% of ad revenue to humanitarian aid

Our Mission

Search for Gaza is a humanitarian search engine inspired by Ecosia. Every search you perform generates ad revenue, and we donate 100% of our share to vetted organizations providing critical aid in Gaza.

We believe that everyday actions can make a real difference. By simply using our search engine for your daily web searches, you're helping provide food, water, medical supplies, and shelter to those in need.

How We Select Charity Partners

We carefully vet each organization to ensure your contributions make the maximum impact:

  • Legitimacy: Verified registration and operational history
  • Transparency: Published financial statements and annual reports
  • Efficiency: Over 80% of funds go directly to programs
  • Focus: Direct humanitarian aid to civilians
  • Compliance: No presence on any watchlists

Your Privacy Matters

Unlike other search engines, we don't track you or build advertising profiles:

  • We don't store your personal information
  • We don't track your search history
  • We don't sell your data to advertisers
  • We use anonymized analytics only to improve our service

Transparency

We publish real-time revenue data and monthly reports showing exactly how much we've donated and to which organizations. Every donation receipt is uploaded for public verification.
